# Fake Personality

## Recognize it
- Emoji sprinkled in headings and buttons
- Quirky microcopy on errors and loading ("Oopsie!", "Hang tight, magic happening ✨")
- A mascot that appears once, with no role
- Random playful illustrations unrelated to the product
- Decorated controls standing in for identity

## Why it is slop
Personality that is not grounded in the product's world reads as borrowed. Cute copy in errors
often hides missing information.

## Repair
- Derive personality from the product's domain and voice (visual-identity).
- Put personality in the environment and key moments, not in every string and control.
- In errors and serious moments, be clear first; personality second, if at all.
- If a mascot exists, give it a defined role and consistent presence.
