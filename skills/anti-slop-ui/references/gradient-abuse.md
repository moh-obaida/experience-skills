# Gradient Abuse

## Recognize it
Purple-to-blue (or pink-to-orange) gradients on: hero backgrounds, headline words, every primary
button, card borders, icons, and "AI" badges. Often combined with blur blobs.

## When gradients have a job
- **Light:** a gradient describing a light source on a surface or scene.
- **Depth:** atmospheric perspective, fading into a background.
- **Signature:** a deliberate brand gradient used in few, consistent places.
- **Data:** continuous scales in visualizations.
- **Scrims:** improving text contrast over images.

## Ask
What does this gradient communicate? Would a flat color lose anything? Is it the same gradient
every AI product uses?

## Repair
- Flat, solid color for buttons and text (easier contrast, clearer state).
- Keep at most one gradient role, tied to light or brand.
- Replace gradient text with type hierarchy (size, weight).
- Check contrast wherever gradients remain behind text.
