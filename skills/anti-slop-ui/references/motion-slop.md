# Motion Slop

## Recognize it
- Every section fades up on scroll
- Cards stagger in on every visit to an app page
- Hover scales every card
- Floating, rotating, or breathing blobs in the background
- Typewriter headlines
- Confetti for trivial actions

## Why it is slop
Motion attached to elements instead of events. It spends attention and time without explaining
anything, and often ignores reduced-motion settings.

## Repair
Remove non-event motion; add event-driven motion where state changes need explaining. See the
motion-design skill and `references/_shared/motion-cliches.md`.
