# Hero Clichés

## Recognize it
- "The [adjective] way to [verb] your [noun]" headline, one word in gradient
- Subhead promising to save time, boost productivity, or unlock potential
- "Get started free" + "Book a demo" buttons
- Tilted product screenshot with glow
- Floating UI fragments or 3D blobs around it
- `100vh` height regardless of content

## Ask
- Could a competitor use this exact headline?
- Does the hero show what the product actually does?
- Does it tell the right visitor they are in the right place within five seconds?

## Repair
- Headline: a specific claim about what the product does for whom. Concrete nouns and verbs.
- Show the product working (real UI, short demo, interactive example) rather than decorative framing.
- One primary action; secondary only if genuinely different.
- Size the hero to its content; bring the next section into view on common laptop heights.
