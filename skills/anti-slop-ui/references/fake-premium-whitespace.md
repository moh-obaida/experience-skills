# Fake-Premium Whitespace

## Recognize it
- Hero sections with 400–600 px of padding around one line
- Narrow content columns floating in wide screens
- Sections separated by more space than they contain
- Tiny light-gray text in the middle of nothing
- Justified as "breathing room," "luxury," "premium"

## Why it is slop
Premium products that use space pair it with exceptional typography, imagery, and precision. Space
without those reads as unfinished. Also: users on 1366×768 laptops scroll through emptiness to
find anything.

## Ask
- What does each large space frame or separate?
- Remove half the space: is anything lost?
- Is type strong enough to hold the space?

## Repair
- Tighten to a spacing scale with clear steps; internal < external spacing.
- Use space to frame one focal element, not everything.
- Strengthen type scale and imagery where space is kept.
- Measure first-viewport coverage (composition-repair's `measure-layout.mjs`) and look at short
  viewports.
