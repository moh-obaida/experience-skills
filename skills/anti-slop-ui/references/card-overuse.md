# Card Overuse

## Recognize it
Every group of content inside a rounded, bordered, shadowed box: paragraphs, stats, settings rows,
form sections, even single sentences. Cards nested in cards.

## When cards are right
Cards represent independent, comparable, often actionable objects: products, projects, games,
people, articles in a feed. They can be scanned, compared, and acted on individually, and often
rearranged.

## When they are wrong
- Prose and editorial content (use headings, rules, and spacing; see
  `references/_shared/editorial-not-cards.md`)
- Settings and forms (use sections with headings and dividers)
- Single values (a number does not need a box)
- Groups that spacing alone could separate

## Ask
Is each card an independent object? Would a list, table, or section heading express it better?

## Repair
- Remove containers; group with spacing and headings.
- Use lists or tables for homogeneous items with several attributes.
- Keep cards for true objects; make their internals consistent (title, meta, action in fixed places).
- Flatten nesting: at most one container level for a group.
