# Centered-Card Syndrome

## Recognize it
Small logo, heading, a card with one or two inputs, a button, all centered; the rest of a large
viewport empty or filled with a flat color or soft gradient. Common on sign-in, join, onboarding,
404, and empty states.

## Why it is slop
- Aligned, not composed; the focal point is "the middle" by default.
- Identity rests on a small logo.
- On large screens, 70–90% of the viewport has no role.
- Reviewers call it "clean," which ends the conversation without evaluating anything.

## Ask
- What is the environment doing? Could the page be any product's?
- Is there a reason the task is tiny relative to the viewport?

## Repair
- Keep the control conventional; invest in the environment (product-derived backdrop, imagery,
  pattern, or a composed split).
- Scale and place the task region deliberately.
- See `references/_shared/join-code-page.md` and the composition-repair skill's alignment guidance.

## When centering is fine
A strongly designed environment frames the centered task (a stage, a full-bleed image, a motif
field). The problem is centering *instead of* design.
