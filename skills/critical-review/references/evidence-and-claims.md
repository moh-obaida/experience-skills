# Evidence and Claims

Match the strength of a claim to the strength of its evidence. The evidence levels E1–E6 are
defined at the end of `references/_shared/experience-core.md`.

## Claim types

| Claim | Needs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | Something seen or measured | "The Join button is at y=812 at 1366×768 (E2)." |
| Inference | Observation + mechanism | "So on common school laptops, students scroll to find it." |
| Prediction | Inference about user behavior | "Some students will think the page is broken." |
| Judgment | Criteria + observations | "The viewport budget is misallocated for a FOCUSED page." |
| Taste | Personal preference | "I'd prefer a warmer palette." |

State which kind each point is, implicitly through wording or explicitly with labels.

## Wording by confidence

- Measured: "is," "does," "fails."
- Reasoned from source: "will," "should," with the reason.
- Predicted behavior: "likely," "users may," with the mechanism.
- Taste: "I prefer," "I'd choose."

## Avoid

- Stating predictions as facts ("users will hate this").
- Hedging observations ("it might possibly overflow" when you measured it).
- Numbers you did not measure presented as measured.
- "Studies show…" without a source.
