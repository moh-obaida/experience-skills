# Product Archetypes and How They Change the Advice

The principles are universal. Their emphasis is not. Use this when the product is not a
typical website or app, or when the advice feels like it is drifting toward "make it a nicer
SaaS dashboard."

## Websites (marketing, portfolio, editorial)

- The environment *is* much of the product. Identity budget is high.
- The job is explanation and persuasion: pacing, storytelling, proof.
- Watch for: hero → features → CTA templates; stock-looking illustration; generic copy.
- Performance matters: heavy assets and scroll effects hurt real visitors.

## Applications (web, mobile, desktop)

- Frequency dominates. Most surfaces are OPERATIONAL, CREATION, or DATA-HEAVY.
- Workflow and state quality usually matter more than ornament.
- Identity lives in type, color roles, rhythm, and interaction feel.
- Watch for: modal overuse, redundant input, missing states, expert tax.

## Games

- Distinct surface types: FOCUSED (join, sign-in), EXPERIENTIAL (play), LIVE CONTROL (host),
  and results/celebration.
- High identity budget; players should feel they entered a world.
- Event-driven motion is central: resolution, transfer, celebration.
- Controls stay simple, especially under time pressure or for young players.
- Watch for: operational patterns in play surfaces; overdesigned join inputs; hosts forced to
  restate context ("Correct for Team A").
- Classroom and party games: legibility at distance, projector contrast, shared-screen privacy.

## Education

- Learners, teachers, and administrators are different audiences with different frequencies.
- Progress, feedback, and motivation are states worth designing.
- Gamification is a tool, not a default; it can trivialize serious learning.
- Teachers are often expert, time-pressed, high-frequency users: workflow compression matters.

## Commerce

- DISCOVERY above, TRANSACTIONAL at the decision point.
- Rich merchandising belongs around the purchase path, not in front of it.
- Known context (region, size history, saved address) should be reused.
- Watch for: modals, promotions, and redundant choices between the user and "Buy."

## Finance and payments

- Trust and safety are the identity. Clarity over spectacle.
- Workflow compression must preserve strong confirmation at money movement.
- State honesty is critical: pending vs completed vs failed must be unambiguous.

## Public service and regulated

- Plain language, accessibility, and task completion dominate.
- Identity is consistency and confidence, not ornament.
- Visual spectacle often harms trust. A plain page can be the excellent answer.
- Some steps are legally required; document them rather than removing them.

## Developer tools and CLIs

- Users are experts who value speed, defaults, and scriptability.
- For CLIs: infer from the project, show the resolved command, allow flags to override,
  never require interactive prompts in non-interactive contexts, make errors actionable,
  report progress for long operations.
- Composition still applies to terminal output: hierarchy, alignment, density, color roles.
- Identity is voice, precision, and speed.

## Data-heavy and monitoring

- The question is "what changed, why, and what needs action."
- Density is appropriate; decoration is not.
- Stale data must be marked; real-time status must be honest.

## Creative tools

- The canvas is the star; chrome recedes.
- Direct manipulation, contextual controls, and keyboard power.
- Progressive complexity: simple first, deep when needed.

## Live control (hosting, broadcasting, operations)

- P0 is current state. Use a state header.
- Actions scoped to known context; big, well-separated targets; undo where possible.
- Glanceability over density; stress-tolerant design.

## Kiosks and shared screens

- No personalization by default; privacy of recent items.
- Large targets; timeouts that reset safely; no hover.
- Visible from a distance.

## Native mobile

- Reach zones, safe areas, platform conventions, on-screen keyboard.
- Bottom sheets and gestures are tools, not defaults.
- Respect system text size and reduced motion.
