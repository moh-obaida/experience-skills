# Brand World

A brand world is the coherent imaginary space a product's surfaces belong to. It gives
designers a way to make new surfaces feel related without copying old ones.

## Defining a world

Answer in a few sentences each:

1. **Setting:** if the product were a place, what place? (a well-lit workshop; a stadium at night;
   a quiet library; a lab bench; a sketchbook)
2. **Materials:** what is it made of? (paper, felt, metal, glass, light, ink, wood, pixels)
3. **Light:** where does light come from, and how does it behave? (soft daylight; stage spotlights;
   screen glow; none, flat)
4. **Geometry:** what shapes recur? (hexes, circles, grids, torn edges, hand-drawn lines)
5. **Energy:** calm, focused, energetic, playful, formal?
6. **Voice:** how does it talk?

## From world to decisions

| World element | Design decisions |
|---|---|
| Setting | Background treatment, imagery subject, layout mood |
| Materials | Surface treatment (texture, flat, layered), borders, shadows |
| Light | Gradients (as light, not decoration), shadows, highlights, dark vs light modes |
| Geometry | Motifs, corner treatments, icon style, pattern |
| Energy | Density, motion speed and easing, color saturation |
| Voice | Copy, naming, error messages |

## Example

A classroom trivia game:

- Setting: a game-show stage in a school gym.
- Materials: painted wood panels, bright vinyl, stage lights.
- Light: warm spotlights from above; the rest of the stage darker.
- Geometry: rounded rectangles like buzzer buttons; hex tiles on the board.
- Energy: high but readable at a distance.
- Voice: friendly host; short sentences; never sarcastic to kids.

Decisions: dark stage background with a warm radial light behind the task area; hex tile motif
in the background field; team colors bright and saturated; standard, large inputs; bold
condensed display type for scores; motion that lands like a buzzer (fast in, small overshoot).

## Keep it grounded

A world is a tool for consistency, not a license for theme-park decoration. Controls stay
usable; text stays legible; performance stays acceptable.
