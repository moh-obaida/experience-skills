# Anti-Slop Gate

For meaningful anti-slop review or repair, judge unjustified generic patterns in this product,
not an aesthetic category. Existing strengths and locked requirements remain constraints. A quiet,
unusual, fashionable, sparse, dense, dark, or panel-based interface can pass after assessment.

## Material assessment

Inspect the whole rendered screen and relevant routes/states at the primary and relevant narrow
viewport. Code signatures and counts are prompts, not verdicts: zero gradients or custom-looking
components do not prove specificity. Ask which pattern materially harms the actual job, hierarchy,
usability, visual quality, interaction character, or route differentiation.

Name the dominant root cause and its level: surface, component, composition, system, identity, or
interaction; several can apply. Group symptoms and cumulative patterns under that cause. Note
secondary contributors and what should remain. Do not output a laundry list of every conventional
badge, radius, or card. Judge product meaning, structural hierarchy, behavior, and expression;
specific copy alone does not earn a transferable skeleton its place.

Useful candidates include emphasis that buries the core instrument, high mass with low useful
value, equal-card grammar that hides semantic priority, identical layouts for different route jobs,
fake metrics/artwork, generic copy, or boilerplate actions, feedback, and uniform decorative motion. None is automatically
wrong. Comparable live instruments may need identical panels; a sparse object page may need quiet
framing; a brand gradient may carry real meaning. Preserve them when their jobs are evidenced.
Accessible native control behavior and a reduced-motion path remain requirements, even when a
treatment has a product reason. Do not use visual novelty to bypass those safeguards.

## Review the original diagnosis

For a meaningful repair, recover the original dominant weakness before evaluating the change.
Compare inspected before/after at the same routes, sizes, and states. Is that weakness materially
reduced at its diagnosed scope? Did a new dominant weakness appear? Is the whole experience more
intentional and still product-specific, with important functionality intact?

A surface cause may be resolved locally. Component, composition, system, identity, or interaction
causes need effects at those levels. A one-line shared rule may achieve systemic improvement;
a large diff or polished component may leave the cause intact. Added copy or controls inside an
oversized secondary container do not resolve its allocation unless the rendered relationship
actually improves. After subtracting a default, check where its useful hierarchy or job should go:
real action, state, instrument, proof, context, or intentionally nothing. Novelty is not a substitute.

## Decision and stop condition

Use an internal **PASS** when no material anti-slop intervention remains justified, **REPAIR** when
a specific root cause remains and can be addressed here, or **ESCALATE** when an unresolved concern
needs another specialist's depth. These are outcomes, not modes or required public labels. A
review-only request diagnoses and recommends; it does not authorize speculative redesign.

If the major original weakness remains, continue repair when feasible or explicitly report it
unresolved. Out-of-scope or accepted major harm is still open; it does not become fixed. Escalate
only for a material structural, identity, interaction, or state concern that this branch cannot
reasonably resolve, using the existing compact handoff. A missing required reference blocks the
branch. Do not activate a fixed specialist bundle.

Rendering unavailable → **UNVERIFIED — NOT VERIFIED IN RENDERED OUTPUT**, with the blocker and
remaining comparison. Edits, browser launch, capture files, fewer markers, or changed copy alone
cannot support a resolved claim. Stop after the material cause visibly improves at its scope,
protected strengths and functionality remain, and no necessary handoff is outstanding; or finish
with an honest unresolved/unverified result. No change is valid after a real assessment.
