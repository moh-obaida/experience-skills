# Precedent: Consequences Visible at the Point of Commitment

Products that show cost, timing, and reversibility next to the action that commits the user.
Observed 2026-09-24; IDs refer to `research/observations/`.

---

### Wise — fees, rate, arrival beside "Send money"
`W1 W3 W5` · https://wise.com/ · rendered + text
- **What works:** amount sent, amount received, guaranteed rate, total fees, and arrival time are all
  in the hero calculator before sign-up; proof claims are specific ("Over 70% arrive in 20 seconds").
- **Lesson:** show every consequence the user will ask about next to the commit action.

### Airbnb — total price including fees
`ABd2 ABm4` · https://www.airbnb.com/ · rendered
- **What works:** prices shown with all fees included (announced to users).
- **Lesson:** removing surprise from the final step is a workflow improvement, not only a policy.

### Spotify — the price after the trial
`SPm1` · https://open.spotify.com/ · rendered 375×812
- **What works:** the trial offer states the monthly price that follows, in local currency.

### Khan Academy — donation amount and frequency
`KA1` · https://www.khanacademy.org/ · rendered
- **What works:** one-time vs recurring and preset amounts are explicit before "Give now".

### Gmail — undo instead of confirm
`GM1` · https://support.google.com/mail/answer/2819488 · documentation
- **What works:** a configurable 5–30 second undo window after send instead of "Are you sure?".
- **Right when:** the action can be held back briefly before it becomes irreversible.

### GOV.UK — once-only actions
`UKD4` · documentation
- **What works:** payments and submissions cannot be repeated via the back button.

### Steam — decision attributes on the capsule
`ST2` · https://store.steampowered.com/ · rendered
- **What works:** price and review summary on the capsule itself.

### Apple — "View pricing" beside "Learn more"
`AP3` · https://www.apple.com/iphone/ · rendered
- **What works:** pricing is one explicit link away in every model panel.

### IKEA — compare on the card
`IK2` · https://www.ikea.com/us/en/ · rendered
- **What works:** a compare checkbox on each product card supports the decision without leaving the list.

### Stripe — live proof figure
`SR2` · https://stripe.com/ · rendered
- **What works:** a live, specific number instead of a generic trust claim.

---

## Summary rules

1. Put cost, timing, and reversibility next to the commit action.
2. Replace pre-action confirmation with a post-action undo window when the effect can be delayed.
3. Make repeat submission impossible after once-only actions.
4. Specific, checkable proof beats generic reassurance.
