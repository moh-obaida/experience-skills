# Observations: Games, Media, and Education

Dated observations of real, public product surfaces made for Experience Skills. Each bullet has a
stable ID that precedent modules cite. Observations describe what was seen; interpretation lives in
the precedent modules (`shared/precedent/`) and the design-intelligence library.

**Method codes:** R = the live page was opened in a browser and looked at (desktop viewport about
800×600 CSS px unless noted; mobile at 375×812); T = page text or documentation read; R+T = both.
Regional versions differ; the region is noted where it affected what was seen. Sites change; treat
every observation as true on its date only. No screenshots are stored in this repository.

## Steam — store front (https://store.steampowered.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- ST1 Dark blue environment with a dense top category nav (Browse, Recommendations, Categories, Ways to Play, Special Sections) and a search field built into the nav bar.
- ST2 Hero is full-bleed key art of a single game with the title logo and one "Now available" action; below, a "Featured & Recommended" carousel of large capsules with price and review summary.
- ST3 Identity is carried by the environment (dark blue gradient field, game art) while controls are ordinary buttons and a search box.

## PlayStation — homepage (https://www.playstation.com/en-us/) — R, 2026-09-24
- PS1 A bright yellow promotional strip for one game sits above a full-bleed key-art hero with headline, one paragraph, and a single "Buy now" action.
- PS2 A row of small game thumbnails below the hero acts as a hero switcher (carousel index as content).
- PS3 A sign-in prompt popover appears over the page on first visit.

## Xbox — homepage (https://www.xbox.com/en-US) — R, 2026-09-24
- XB1 The first viewport is an uncropped photographic campaign image (athletes in a dark studio) with no headline visible above the fold at 800×600; the message is deferred below the image.
- XB2 A floating "Need help? Let's chat" pill overlaps the lower-right corner of the hero.

## Nintendo — homepage (https://www.nintendo.com/us/) — R, 2026-09-24
- NI1 First paint was a blank white page with only the red header and a floating bottom action bar for more than 3 seconds before the hero art appeared: loading state indistinguishable from empty.
- NI2 Navigation is a floating pill-shaped bar at the bottom center (menu, wishlist, search emphasized as a red circle, cart, account) even on a desktop-width viewport; search is the visually primary action.
- NI3 Hero is full-bleed illustrated key art of one game with no text in the first viewport.

## Discord — homepage (https://discord.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- DI1 Deep blue/violet starfield environment populated with illustrated characters and floating objects; heavy uppercase display headline; the world is playful and specific to gaming/hangouts.
- DI2 Primary action is OS-aware ("Download for Mac") with an alternative "Open Discord in your browser": platform inferred, choice preserved.
- DI3 The product UI appears inside device mockups placed within the illustrated scene rather than as a flat screenshot.

## Codecademy — homepage (https://www.codecademy.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- CC1 A countdown timer banner for a sale sits above the navigation (urgency pattern, persistent across the visit).
- CC2 The headline is set in white on black blocks with a yellow highlight, a slash and a text cursor ("/skills|"): a code-editor motif derived from the product.
- CC3 "Sign up with Google" is the first sign-up option, placed over the hero photo.

## Brilliant — homepage (https://brilliant.org/) — R, 2026-09-24
- BR1 Mostly white page with a centered serif headline and a single geometric puzzle illustration that looks like an actual lesson problem (squares, dotted guides, a pointer): the hero visual is the product's own content type.
- BR2 Very little chrome (logo, Sign in); awards shown as small badges under the tagline.

## Coursera — homepage (https://www.coursera.org/) — R, 2026-09-24
- CO1 Hero is a carousel of partner-branded offers; below, dense columns ("Most popular", "Hot new releases", "Trending AI courses") each listing courses with provider logo, type (Professional Certificate / Course), and rating.
- CO2 Provider logos (Google, AWS, Microsoft) act as the main trust and scanning signal in list rows.

## Khan Academy — homepage (https://www.khanacademy.org/) — R, 2026-09-24
- KA1 A donation module is the first-viewport task: frequency (One time / Recurring, Monthly / Yearly) and preset amounts ($12, $20, $35, $75, Other) with one "Give now" action. Presets are smart defaults for an otherwise blank amount field.
- KA2 A top banner routes an audience ("For teachers: sign up free to assign practice"), separating teacher onboarding from learner content.
- KA3 Cookie dialog offers "Strictly Necessary Only" as a full-width button equal to "Accept All Cookies".

## Spotify — web player, mobile (https://open.spotify.com/) — R at 375×812, 2026-09-24
- SPm1 A Premium upsell card is the first element, with the trial price in local currency (AED) and the post-trial price stated; "Open App" is the header's primary action.
- SPm2 Horizontal shelves of square artwork ("Trending songs", "Popular artists") are the entire layout; a cookie sheet covers the lower third on arrival.

## Kahoot — player join page (https://kahoot.it/) — R, 2026-09-24
- KH1 Full-bleed saturated purple field with large soft geometric shapes; white logo; a small white panel with one "Game PIN" input and a dark "Join" button.
- KH2 The only control is a standard text input: no segmented digit boxes. Personality is entirely in the environment; the page is legible across a classroom.

## Blooket — player join page (https://play.blooket.com/play) — R, 2026-09-24
- BK1 Teal field covered in a repeated pattern of tilted, rounded, translucent squares; a large rounded display wordmark; one "Game ID" input with an arrow submit button.
- BK2 A single repeated motif plus a type voice creates an unmistakable world with no decorated controls.

## Duolingo — homepage (https://www.duolingo.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- DU1 The owl character inside a phone-shaped illustration is the focal image; a short headline; two stacked actions ("Get started" filled, "I already have an account" outlined) — returning users get an equally visible path.

## Spotify — web player, desktop (https://open.spotify.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- SPd1 Signed-out library sidebar shows short empty states with one action each ("Create your first playlist · It's easy, we'll help you · Create playlist"; "Let's find some podcasts to follow · Browse podcasts").
- SPd2 Dense shelves of square artwork ("Trending songs", "Popular artists") on a dark environment.
