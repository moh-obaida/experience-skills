# Observations: Commerce, Travel, and Finance

Dated observations of real, public product surfaces made for Experience Skills. Each bullet has a
stable ID that precedent modules cite. Observations describe what was seen; interpretation lives in
the precedent modules (`shared/precedent/`) and the design-intelligence library.

**Method codes:** R = the live page was opened in a browser and looked at (desktop viewport about
800×600 CSS px unless noted; mobile at 375×812); T = page text or documentation read; R+T = both.
Regional versions differ; the region is noted where it affected what was seen. Sites change; treat
every observation as true on its date only. No screenshots are stored in this repository.

## Wise — homepage (https://wise.com/) — R+T, 2026-09-24, regional (AE) version
- W1 The live transfer quote is the hero's working object: "You send exactly" AED 10,000 prefilled, recipient amount, arrival "Today - in seconds", total fees "Included" shown before any sign-up. Product-first transactional hero.
- W2 Source currency defaulted from location (AED on the UAE site); rate shown with "Rate is guaranteed" at the point of decision. Known context reused.
- W3 Consequence facts (fee amount, rate, arrival time) sit next to the primary action, not in a later step or footnote.
- W4 Strong flat brand field (bright lime) with heavy condensed uppercase display type; the calculator panel is a plain dark/white form. Environment carries identity, controls stay conventional.
- W5 Proof claims are specific and checkable ("Over 70% arrive in 20 seconds", mid-market rate explanation) rather than generic "fast and secure".

## Booking.com — homepage (https://www.booking.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- B1 A sign-in/membership modal covers the search on arrival; the primary task is blocked by a promotional interruption.
- B2 Search is a single row: destination, dates, guests, Search; plus a "I'm traveling for work" checkbox that changes results without a separate flow.
- B3 Below the search, a row of four icon-topped benefit cards ("Book now, pay at the property", "300M+ reviews"...) — a generic benefit-card pattern.
- B4 Currency shown as AED in the header (inferred region) with a flag control for changing it.

## Google Flights — search start (https://www.google.com/travel/flights) — R, 2026-09-24
- G1 Origin prefilled with the user's city (Dubai); trip type, passengers, and cabin default to Round trip / 1 / Economy as compact inline dropdowns above the main fields.
- G2 A light line illustration fills the top environment; the search form itself is entirely standard fields.
- G3 Single primary action ("Explore") centered under the form; dates left empty (genuinely unknown).
- G4 Secondary AI entry point presented as a separate, clearly labeled panel below the task rather than replacing the form.

## IKEA — BILLY series/category page (https://www.ikea.com/us/en/) — R, 2026-09-24
- IK1 Header carries "Enter ZIP code" and "Select store": availability and delivery depend on location, so location is a persistent, editable context rather than a checkout-time question.
- IK2 Listing uses plain Sort / Size / Color filter controls above the grid and a "Compare" checkbox on every product card: comparison is a first-class discovery action.
- IK3 Product imagery on flat light backgrounds; "Best seller" badges as the only decoration on cards.
- IK4 A cookie banner and a chat invitation popup both overlay the first viewport simultaneously (interruption stack).

## Patagonia — homepage (https://www.patagonia.com/home/) — R, 2026-09-24
- PA1 The first viewport is an activism campaign ("Vote Big") on full-bleed black with a large serif display line and a single outlined "Sign Up" action, before any product. The brand's values are the hero.
- PA2 Product categories appear only below the fold as a row of image tiles; commerce is present but deliberately second.

## Revolut — homepage (https://www.revolut.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- RV1 Full-bleed sky photograph; left-aligned headline and one action ("Download the app"); a phone-shaped card overlaid on a portrait photo shows a real balance ("£6,012 · Accounts"): the product appears inside a lifestyle image rather than as a device screenshot.
- RV2 A thin top banner advertises a local promotion (Revolut coffee event): regional content above navigation.

## Airbnb — home, mobile (https://www.airbnb.com/) — R at 375×812, 2026-09-24
- ABm1 Search collapses to one large pill ("Start your search") that expands into the full form: the most frequent action is one thumb-sized target.
- ABm2 A bottom tab bar (Explore, Wishlists, Log in) holds top-level navigation within thumb reach; category chips (All, Homes, Experiences) scroll horizontally under the search.
- ABm3 Content rows scroll horizontally ("Explore experiences nearby", "Available in Palm Jumeirah this weekend"): location and dates inferred into section titles.
- ABm4 A floating pill ("Prices include all fees") confirms a policy change inline instead of the desktop modal; an app-install banner sits at the top.

## Stripe — homepage (https://stripe.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- SR1 The headline uses two tones: the first phrase in near-black, the rest of the sentence in mid-grey, creating hierarchy inside one paragraph without a separate subhead.
- SR2 A live-updating figure ("Global GDP running on Stripe: 1.7310…%") sits above the headline: a specific, changing proof point instead of a generic claim.
- SR3 A flowing multi-color gradient ribbon fills the right edge of the hero; it is the brand's long-running signature, used as environment rather than on buttons, which are solid.
- SR4 Cookie banner offers "Accept all" and "Reject all" side by side.

## Apple — iPhone page (https://www.apple.com/iphone/) — R, 2026-09-24
- AP1 A dismissible banner asks the visitor to confirm or change the detected region ("United Arab Emirates · Continue") rather than silently redirecting.
- AP2 A very large "iPhone" heading, then a horizontal model navigator of product thumbnails with "New" labels: the model range is the navigation.
- AP3 Model sections are rounded panels with a short headline ("iPhone Duo — Hello, hello."), one filled "Learn more" and one text link "View pricing"; the product image is the focal weight.
- AP4 The page was blank white for about 3 seconds before content appeared.

## Monzo — homepage (https://monzo.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- MZ1 The hero photo shows a hand holding a phone with the coral Monzo card on screen: the signature card color is the brand motif, placed inside a real-life image.
- MZ2 An independent recommendation badge (Which? Recommended Provider) is placed on the hero image as a trust signal.
- MZ3 Cookie dialog offers "Accept all", "Reject all", and "Customise" at equal prominence, in plain language ("We're not talking about the crunchy, tasty kind").

## Aesop — homepage (https://www.aesop.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- AE1 Warm off-white canvas; an editorial hero introduces a fragrance collection as a story with an "Explore the story" action; restrained type and color; curated product sets below.

## Nike — homepage (https://www.nike.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- NK1 Redirected to a regional store from location; dense top navigation with search; a full-bleed campaign hero with a large condensed headline and two actions (Shop, Explore Running).

## Airbnb — homepage, desktop (https://www.airbnb.com/) — R, 2026-09-24
- ABd1 Search split into Where / When / Who is the focal element; large photography in category rows; region inferred from location (redirect to regional domain).
- ABd2 An arrival modal announces total-price display ("one price for your trip, all fees included") over the search task.
