# Visual Reference Catalog

Products worth studying for *why* they work. This is not a list of things to copy. Every entry
says what the lesson is, where it applies, and what would go wrong if it were transplanted.

**Observed 2026-09-24.** Sites change often; re-check before relying on a detail.

**Observation method** for each entry:

- **R** (rendered): the live page was opened in a browser and looked at.
- **T** (text): the live page's content and structure were read as text; visual claims are limited
  to what the text shows.
- **F** (familiarity): not re-observed in this pass; the entry relies on general knowledge of the
  product and should be verified.

No screenshots or assets are included. All commentary is original.

## Apple — iPhone product page
- **Category:** marketing, commerce · **URL:** https://www.apple.com/iphone/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a model navigator at the top, then a long sequence of themed chapters (durability,
  cameras, intelligence, environment, privacy, safety), each with one or two actions ("Learn more",
  "Buy"), followed by shopping reassurance (trade-in, financing, delivery).
- **Strengths:** pacing (one idea per chapter), strong hierarchy, consistent paired actions, rich
  imagery with plain controls.
- **Lesson:** high visual richness can coexist with extremely simple controls. The drama is in the
  environment and the storytelling; the buttons are ordinary.
- **Do not copy blindly:** long scroll storytelling into utility workflows. A settings page or an
  admin tool gains nothing from chaptered theatre.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, composition-repair, motion-design · **Archetypes:** marketing, DISCOVERY

## Stripe — homepage
- **Category:** developer / financial infrastructure marketing · **URL:** https://stripe.com/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a benefit headline about revenue infrastructure, customer logos, a grid of product
  areas with background imagery, a scale-statistics band, segment sections (enterprise, startups,
  platforms) with case studies, and a developer section offering several integration paths.
- **Strengths:** a technical product explained through outcomes and proof; statistics that carry
  context (currencies, volume, uptime) rather than vanity; different entry points per audience.
- **Lesson:** statistics can be good content when they are proof of a specific claim. Compare with
  meaningless stat cards on an empty dashboard.
- **Do not copy blindly:** the layered marketing polish into product surfaces; or statistics for
  their own sake.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, anti-slop-ui (as the counter-example to fake stats) · **Archetypes:** marketing

## Linear — homepage and product
- **Category:** productivity / developer tool · **URL:** https://linear.app/ · **Method:** T (product: F)
- **Observed:** a positioning headline, real product interface imagery, value propositions around
  being purpose-built and fast, feature areas each shown with product mockups, customer quotes, and
  a changelog preview.
- **Strengths:** product shown directly; speed as a stated value; changelog as proof of momentum.
  The product itself is known for dense lists, muted metadata, keyboard-first operation, and
  restrained color.
- **Lesson:** density and speed can feel refined when hierarchy is disciplined and interaction is
  fast. Identity comes from interaction feel and restraint, not ornament.
- **Do not copy blindly:** the dark, minimal SaaS aesthetic. On products without Linear's audience,
  frequency, and keyboard culture, it reads as generic "devtool" styling.
- **Skills:** interaction-design, workflow-compression, composition-repair · **Archetypes:** OPERATIONAL, CREATION

## Raycast — homepage
- **Category:** developer productivity · **URL:** https://www.raycast.com/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a short headline about being a shortcut to everything, a prominent keyboard visual,
  an extensions showcase by category, AI features, automation features (snippets, hotkeys), a
  community section, and a developer API section. Reliability is stated as a metric.
- **Strengths:** the keyboard, the product's core interaction, is the visual motif. Identity comes
  from the mechanic.
- **Lesson:** product-derived motifs. A launcher's identity is the keyboard and speed; showing it is
  more distinctive than an abstract hero graphic.
- **Do not copy blindly:** dark presentation and glow effects without a product reason.
- **Skills:** visual-identity · **Archetypes:** marketing

## Framer — homepage
- **Category:** creative tool · **URL:** https://www.framer.com/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a positioning headline about designing from idea to launch, sections demonstrating
  generation, CMS, code, and integrations with live design variations, and platform capabilities
  (performance, hosting, SEO).
- **Strengths:** the marketing site demonstrates the product's own capabilities (expressive layout,
  motion, typography), so the medium is the proof.
- **Lesson:** for creative tools, expressive layout is evidence, not decoration.
- **Do not copy blindly:** experimental typography and motion into operational software where users
  need calm and speed.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, motion-design · **Archetypes:** marketing, CREATION

## Vercel — homepage
- **Category:** developer platform · **URL:** https://vercel.com/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a short, typographic presentation: headline, two actions, positioning statement,
  customer use cases with metrics and feature bullets, recent launches, and a closing call to action.
- **Strengths:** restraint; typography and grid carry the brand; consistency between marketing and
  documentation.
- **Lesson:** monochrome, grid-based minimalism works when typographic precision and consistency are
  real and the audience values them.
- **Do not copy blindly:** black-and-white minimalism without that precision produces generic
  AI-devtool slop. This is one of the most copied looks and among the least distinctive when copied.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, anti-slop-ui · **Archetypes:** marketing, CONTENT (docs)

## Figma — homepage and editor
- **Category:** creative tool · **URL:** https://www.figma.com/ · **Method:** R (editor: F)
- **Observed:** a large, stacked typographic headline about a creative canvas, a single "get started"
  action, and a release-notes banner.
- **Strengths (editor):** direct manipulation, contextual property panels, progressive complexity
  (simple shapes first, auto layout and variables later), clear spatial hierarchy with the canvas
  as the star.
- **Lesson:** in creation tools, chrome recedes and contextual controls replace modal dialogs.
- **Do not copy blindly:** a dense, panel-heavy editor layout into a simple content form.
- **Skills:** interaction-design, composition-repair · **Archetypes:** CREATION

## Notion — homepage and product
- **Category:** productivity · **URL:** https://www.notion.com/ · **Method:** R (product: F)
- **Observed:** a large headline with an inline pill-shaped word treatment, two actions, a product
  screenshot, and a customer logo strip. The product is known for its illustration style used in
  marketing.
- **Strengths (product):** familiar controls, inline editing everywhere, slash commands as a power
  path, progressive disclosure through hover handles and menus, very little persistent chrome.
- **Lesson:** flexibility without constant UI noise: power is available at the point of need.
- **Do not copy blindly:** hover-only affordances; they need keyboard and touch equivalents.
- **Skills:** interaction-design, workflow-compression · **Archetypes:** CREATION, OPERATIONAL

## Airbnb — homepage
- **Category:** travel marketplace · **URL:** https://www.airbnb.com/ · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** the request was redirected to a regional domain based on location; a search bar
  split into where / when / who is the focal element; categories of stays and services with large
  photography; an announcement modal on arrival about showing total prices including fees.
- **Strengths:** search-first; a complex decision (place, dates, guests) reduced to three plain
  fields; imagery carries identity; region inferred rather than asked.
- **Lesson:** reduce complex choices to a few familiar inputs, and let the content (photography) be
  the identity.
- **Do not copy blindly:** arrival modals. The price-transparency message is valuable, but a modal
  interrupts the primary task; weigh it against inline messaging.
- **Skills:** workflow-compression, interaction-design, visual-identity · **Archetypes:** DISCOVERY

## Spotify — web player
- **Category:** media, consumer · **URL:** https://open.spotify.com/ · **Method:** R (signed out)
- **Observed:** dark environment; a library sidebar whose empty state says what to do and offers one
  action ("create your first playlist", "find podcasts to follow"); dense rows of trending songs and
  popular artists with large artwork.
- **Strengths:** dense content organized in scannable shelves; artwork carries identity; empty
  library states are short and actionable rather than decorative.
- **Lesson:** a real example of a meaningful empty state: explain, offer one next action, nothing more.
- **Do not copy blindly:** the dark media aesthetic onto document-heavy or form-heavy products.
- **Skills:** empty-state-design, composition-repair · **Archetypes:** DISCOVERY, EXPERIENTIAL

## Duolingo — homepage
- **Category:** education, consumer · **URL:** https://www.duolingo.com/ · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** the owl character on a phone-shaped illustration, a short headline about the most fun
  way to learn languages and more, and two clear actions (get started / already have an account).
- **Strengths:** a product-specific character language used consistently; playful identity with very
  plain actions; strong event feedback and progression in the product (F).
- **Lesson:** character and celebration can drive motivation when tied to real progress, while the
  controls stay simple.
- **Do not copy blindly:** gamification (streaks, mascots, confetti) in finance, health, or serious
  tools, where it can feel manipulative or trivializing.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, motion-design, state-design · **Archetypes:** EXPERIENTIAL, FOCUSED

## Kahoot — player join page
- **Category:** education game · **URL:** https://kahoot.it/ · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** a full-bleed saturated purple background with large, soft geometric shapes; the logo
  in white; a small white panel with a single "Game PIN" input and a dark "Join" button; links to
  the main site below.
- **Strengths:** unmistakable environment; the control is utterly standard; legible at a glance.
- **Lesson:** the canonical case of *interesting environment + simple input*. The personality is in
  the page, not in the PIN field.
- **Do not copy blindly:** the saturated, high-energy look into calm or official contexts.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, interaction-design, composition-repair · **Archetypes:** FOCUSED

## Blooket — player join page
- **Category:** education game · **URL:** https://play.blooket.com/play · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** a teal background covered in a pattern of tilted, rounded, translucent square shapes;
  a large rounded display wordmark; a single "Game ID" input with an arrow submit button; a cookie
  consent panel on first visit.
- **Strengths:** a recognizable visual world from a simple repeated motif; one input; zero clutter.
- **Lesson:** personality does not require complicated controls. A pattern and a type voice are enough.
- **Do not copy blindly:** motif density that reduces contrast around the control.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, anti-slop-ui · **Archetypes:** FOCUSED

## GOV.UK — service guidance page
- **Category:** public service · **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/renew-adult-passport · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** a cookie banner with equal-weight accept and reject; the GOV.UK header; breadcrumbs;
  a large bold heading in plain language; a contents list for the multi-part guide.
- **Strengths:** clarity, plain language, accessibility, predictable structure across the whole
  site, consent choices presented fairly. The GOV.UK Design System documents patterns such as one
  question per page for complex services and error summaries.
- **Lesson:** good experience design does not require flashy visuals. Beauty here is clarity,
  confidence, and speed. Plainness applied consistently is an identity.
- **Do not copy blindly:** the one-question-per-page pattern for short forms where a single page is
  faster (see the public-service form example); and do not turn a service into Kahoot.
- **Skills:** critical-review, interaction-design, workflow-compression · **Archetypes:** TRANSACTIONAL, CONTENT

## Aesop — homepage
- **Category:** commerce, editorial · **URL:** https://www.aesop.com/ · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** a warm off-white canvas, an editorial hero introducing a fragrance collection as a
  story with an "explore the story" action, then curated product sets. Restrained type and color.
- **Strengths:** editorial identity; commerce presented through narrative and restraint; consistent
  material logic (flat, warm, printed feel).
- **Lesson:** restraint works when every remaining element is precise: type, imagery, and copy carry
  the brand.
- **Do not copy blindly:** editorial restraint into operational tools, or as an excuse for low
  information density on product-decision pages.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, composition-repair · **Archetypes:** DISCOVERY, CONTENT

## Nike — homepage
- **Category:** commerce, campaign · **URL:** https://www.nike.com/ · **Method:** R
- **Observed:** redirected to a regional store; dense top navigation with search; a promotional
  strip; a full-bleed campaign hero with a large condensed headline and two actions (shop, explore
  running); a cookie consent banner.
- **Strengths:** campaign expression through photography and bold type; merchandising entry points
  immediately available; region inferred.
- **Lesson:** dynamic campaign identity can sit on top of a conventional, efficient commerce structure.
- **Do not copy blindly:** editorial campaign drama into admin software or checkout steps.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, workflow-compression · **Archetypes:** DISCOVERY, marketing

## Additional categories

### Data-heavy — Our World in Data
- **URL:** https://ourworldindata.org/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a mission headline, thousands of charts organized into topic pages, data explorers,
  short "data insights", popular pages, search, and a large topic directory.
- **Lesson:** density organized hierarchically. Many entry points, each specific; charts chosen per
  question.
- **Do not copy:** encyclopedic navigation into a focused tool.
- **Skills:** composition-repair, anti-slop-ui (versus KPI-card dashboards)

### Editorial — The Pudding
- **URL:** https://pudding.cool/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a chronological list of visual stories, each with issue number, date, thumbnail,
  headline, and a conversational subtitle; playful sticker-like touches.
- **Lesson:** strong voice in headlines and a simple list can carry an editorial identity. The stories
  themselves are where visual invention lives.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, composition-repair

### Documentation — MDN Web Docs
- **URL:** https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/grid-template-columns · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** breadcrumbs, hierarchical sidebar, a support-status badge, an interactive example,
  then syntax, values, formal definition, examples, specifications, compatibility, and related links.
- **Lesson:** predictable structure per page type lets experts jump directly to what they need;
  interactive examples come before formal detail.
- **Skills:** composition-repair, product-friction (information architecture)

### Finance — Monzo
- **URL:** https://monzo.com/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a lifestyle-oriented headline, app and card imagery built around a signature card
  color, a repeated single primary action (open an account), and prominent trust signals (deposit
  protection, switching guarantee, independent recommendation).
- **Lesson:** a signature color and plain, reassuring copy build identity in a trust-critical domain;
  trust signals are content, not decoration.
- **Skills:** visual-identity, critical-review

### Ultra-utilitarian — Hacker News
- **URL:** https://news.ycombinator.com/ · **Method:** T
- **Observed:** a numbered list of text links with domain, points, author, age, and comment counts;
  no images; minimal styling.
- **Lesson:** for an expert, high-frequency audience, extreme density and plainness can be the
  right product decision, and the plainness itself becomes recognizable.
- **Do not copy:** into consumer or first-time-user contexts; small tap targets and low contrast are
  real costs on mobile.
- **Skills:** composition-repair, critical-review

### Mobile and native
Native apps were not observed in this pass. For platform conventions (navigation, safe areas,
Dynamic Type, touch targets), refer to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines
(https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) and Material Design
(https://m3.material.io/). The responsive-validation and interaction-design skills summarize the
relevant checks.

## Using this catalog

When referencing a product in advice:

1. Name the specific decision, not the vibe.
2. State why it works for that product (audience, frequency, density, brand).
3. Check whether the target product shares those conditions.
4. Translate the principle; do not copy the surface.
