# Scenario: Preserve Purposeful Negative Space

## Scenario
An invented ceramic-object editorial page uses sparse typography and a vessel silhouette. Empty space isolates the object and paces attention; a single materials link is easy to find.
All scenes are invented in `visual-relationships.html`; `space` is the target view.

## Prompt
Render the target view with `node .benchmark/render.mjs before relationships.html --views space` and inspect the captures. Critically review `relationships.html#space` for visual allocation and hierarchy. Render and inspect the view. The sparse concept and identity are intentional. Recommend changes only for observed material problems; do not edit.

## Mode
review

## Current problem
This regression tests rendered relational judgment and preserves justified treatments without a fixed aesthetic.

## Expected skills
- critical-review

## Key principles expected
- Evaluate what negative space frames and how the object, headline, and link relate; do not equate low content coverage with failure.
- Preserve sparse dramatic framing when its product purpose and usable hierarchy are supported.
- Permit a no-change verdict after inspection and avoid automatically routing to creation specialists.

## Unacceptable recommendations
- Filling empty regions with stats, cards, tips, or extra CTAs.
- Compressing the page merely because it is sparse or replacing its identity.
