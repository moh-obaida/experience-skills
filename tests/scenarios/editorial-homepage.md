# Scenario: Editorial Homepage

## Scenario
An independent magazine's homepage presents every article as an identical rounded card in a
three-column grid with an icon, a pill for the category, and a gradient overlay. The editor says it
looks "like a SaaS dashboard, not a magazine."

## Prompt
Our independent magazine's homepage shows every article as the same rounded card in a three-column grid, each with an icon, a category pill, and a gradient overlay on the image. Our editor says it looks like a SaaS dashboard, not a magazine. How should we fix it?

## Current problem
Card overuse; flat hierarchy; no editorial identity; pill and gradient slop.

## Expected skills
- composition-repair
- visual-identity
- anti-slop-ui

## Key principles expected
- Editorial hierarchy: lead story, secondary stories, sections; typography carries identity.
- Imagery with art direction rather than gradient overlays.
- Cards only where items are genuinely independent and comparable.
- Logo test on the homepage and an article page.

## Unacceptable recommendations
- Switching to a bento grid.
- Adding more pills, icons, or glass effects "for personality".
- Keeping the uniform grid and only changing colors.
