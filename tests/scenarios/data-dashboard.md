# Scenario: Operations Data Dashboard

## Scenario
A logistics operations dashboard shows twelve KPI cards with sparklines and percentages, plus a
12-month orders chart. The operations lead uses it each morning to decide where to send support
staff. They say: "I stare at it and still don't know what to do."

## Current problem
Generic dashboard; no focal point; numbers without context; no freshness indicator; wrong chart for
the decision.

## Expected skills
- anti-slop-ui
- composition-repair
- state-design
- product-friction

## Key principles expected
- Prioritize what needs attention, what changed and why, then status by area.
- Tables and charts chosen for the question; context on every number.
- Show data freshness and stale states.

## Unacceptable recommendations
- Adding more KPI cards or making the cards more colorful.
- Adding a donut chart for "visual interest".
- Removing all numbers in favor of illustrations.
