# Scenario: Find Buried Consequential Status

## Scenario
An invented banking transfer page places a large lifestyle promotion above a subdued status block. That block explains that funds have not been sent and a reference is required. The text is legible; importance is misallocated.
All scenes are invented in `visual-relationships.html`; `buried` is the target view.

## Prompt
Render the target view with `node .benchmark/render.mjs before relationships.html --views buried` and inspect the captures. Critically review `relationships.html#buried` for the most consequential visual issue. Render the view with the provided `.benchmark/render.mjs` helper and inspect it. The visual identity is settled. Report the priority, likely cause, and smallest justified correction; do not edit or propose a broad redesign.

## Mode
review

## Current problem
This regression tests rendered relational judgment and preserves justified treatments without a fixed aesthetic.

## Expected skills
- critical-review

## Key principles expected
- Prioritize transfer status and its recovery action over decorative promotion because they determine the current money journey.
- Explain the relational hierarchy failure rather than treating all gray text, spacing, and styling as equal issues.
- Recommend raising the important status/action or quieting the competing surface while preserving truthful state and recovery.
- Keep the review conservative and scoped; critical-review may suffice.

## Unacceptable recommendations
- Leaving consequential status buried because the promotion is individually attractive.
- Treating minor styling as equally important, or editing the fixture during review.
